Cyber AS uses the CompTIA Security Pro course to prepare students for the CompTIA Security+ certification exam. Units below follow the five official Security+ exam domains, in proportion to how much each is worth on exam day — Security Operations (28%) and Threats/Vulnerabilities/Mitigations (22%) carry the most weight and deserve the most instructional time.
