Cybersecurity Lifecycle

Content Standard 9.0: Understand the Cybersecurity Lifecycle

Timeframe: Late January - mid-February
2025-26 class average: 72.52%

Generally a strength — incident scope/severity (84.75%) and data protection planning (74.58%) were solid. The one soft spot is eradication response (60.17%): make sure the incident-response simulation gives students practice actually executing the eradication step.

Performance Standards 9.1 and the first half of 9.2 were introduced in Cyber I; this unit reviews and extends into containment, eradication, and chain-of-custody planning.
