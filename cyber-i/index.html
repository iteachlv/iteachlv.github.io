Cyber I

Year 1 · Foundations of the Cybersecurity program of study

Cyber I lays the groundwork for the whole pathway: shop safety, hardware, legal/ethical issues, operating systems, and the network theory that Cyber II builds on. Standards 5.0 and 6.0 (Operating Systems and Network Theory) are co-taught across both years — introduced here, then deepened and tested in Cyber II.

units

UNIT 1

Safety Procedures & Tools

Shop and lab safety, secure disposal of technology materials, and proper use of industry-standard diagnostic and repair tools.

 UNIT 2

Hardware Components

Identifying, installing, and configuring core hardware — motherboards, storage, audio/video, and printers.

 UNIT 3

Legal, Technical & Ethical Issues

Legal and ethical issues in technology, privacy, and the fundamentals of customer relations. This standard scored 100% on last year's state exam — keep it that way with a light-touch review before testing in Cyber II.

 UNIT 4

Operating Systems

Installing, securing, and troubleshooting operating systems — first exposure here.

 UNIT 5

Industry Standards, Practices & Network Theory

The OSI model, numbering systems, and networking fundamentals that everything in Cyber II builds on.

 UNIT 6

Cybersecurity Lifecycle — Foundations

An introduction to the cybersecurity lifecycle and the early stages of incident response planning.

 UNIT 7

Computer Forensics — Foundations

Threat actors, attack types, and an introduction to digital forensics response tools.

 UNIT 8

Emerging Technologies

Workforce and society needs related to new and emerging technologies, and the ethics involved in securing them.
