About Me

I'm a proud Las Vegas native and a Career & Technical Education Curriculum Leader for the Clark County School District, where I have designed and built cybersecurity, computer science, IT, and web design courses taught across the district — built out in Canvas LMS and aligned to Nevada's End-of-Course assessments and CompTIA certification frameworks.

I started in the classroom teaching AP U.S. Government and AP Comparative Politics, then moved into instructional design — training staff on new platforms and tools before eventually building full curricula from the ground up. Along the way, I picked up two master's degrees and a run of doctoral coursework in Curriculum & Instruction, plus Nevada licenses spanning Computer Science, Computer-Based Applications, Social Studies, and School Administration.

I coach FBLA to state and national recognition most years, and the work has earned a few honors along the way, including an NCWIT Inspirations Educator Award and a Vegas Inc. Top Tech Award for Education.

Off the clock, I'm training for my next ultramarathon out in Red Rock Canyon, and keeping up with my teenage twins — who keep me at least as busy as any curriculum deadline.
